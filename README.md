# TGG Breaks Into the GTA 6 Military Base — make.fun gta6-clipper-01

My entry for the make.fun bounty **"Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views"** ($500).

## The video

- **YouTube Short:** https://youtube.com/shorts/zzWKHaCg8us
- **Channel:** Happy and Funny
- **Moment:** TGG breaks into the Mile Zero military base in GTA 6 — spike strips,
  soldiers, and a jet heist gone wrong. The clip is taken from TGG's GTA 6 launch
  stream, 3:05:14–3:05:54
  (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMXp80Cntxw).

## How it was made

My AI agent found the moment in the stream, cut it into a 40-second vertical
Short, and rendered it with a pitch for make.fun at the end: the clip tells
viewers they can earn money on make.fun by making cool clips with AI.

## The entry

- `VIDEO.json` names the YouTube Short above.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `VIDEO.json` to the
  output directory, as the bounty requires.
- Places are decided by YouTube views, most first; the video stays public
  until the results are out.
